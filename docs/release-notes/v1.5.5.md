Scan text inserts by itself in Messages and WhatsApp.

### Fixed
- **Insert text** now waits a moment for your messaging app to finish settling before typing the scanned text in. Apps like Google Messages and WhatsApp restore their saved draft just after you return, which was wiping text typed in straight away. The keyboard checks the text stuck and tries once more if not.
- If it still can't get in, the **Insert scanned text** button appears in the suggestion bar as before. It stays there until you tap it.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
