Fixes for two-letter words and double letters in glide typing.

### Fixed
- **"I'd" taking over**: a context rule meant to tell *I'd* from *id* was lifting *I'd* above unrelated words, so swiping *of*, *is* or *it* often gave *I'd*. It now only ever ranks one of that pair lower.
- **Two-letter words**: for words that are just a straight line between two keys, where you start and end and the direction of the swipe now count for more.

### New
- **Double letters, Swype-style**: draw a small loop on a letter to double it: swipe o → loop on f for *off*, t → loop on o for *too*, f → loop on e → l for *feel*.
- **Twin words always on offer**: when two words trace exactly the same keys (*of*/*off*, *to*/*too*), the other one is always right next to your word in the suggestion bar.
- **Learns from your fixes**: when you swap a swiped word for another suggestion, the one you rejected ranks a little lower next time.

In tests on simulated swipes, short and double-letter words went from about 83% to 95% right first time with careful swipes, and from 66% to 74% with sloppy ones.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
