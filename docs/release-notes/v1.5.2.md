Scan text inserts into your message again.

### Fixed
- **Insert text** from Scan text now lands in the message box you were typing in. The scanned text was being handed to the Scan text screen's own text box as it closed, so it never reached your app. When you're back in your app the text appears at the cursor (if the keyboard doesn't open by itself, tap the message box).

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
