Swype-style glide typing: more accurate, especially when you swipe fast or sloppily.

### New
- **Turns and pauses count**: the keyboard now records the timing of your swipe and finds the spots where your finger turned sharply or slowed down. Those are treated as the letters you meant, and the drift across keys in between counts for less. Words whose letters line up with your turns rank higher.
- **Retuned shape matching**: the overall shape of the swipe matters most; a slightly off start or end, cut corners and wobble cost less.
- **Context**: the word before helps pick the right one ("more th…" → *than*, "over th…" → *there*), alongside how often you use each word. Next-word predictions use the same, larger set of common phrases.

In tests on simulated swipes, sloppy swipes went from about 68% to 81% right first time, fast swipes from 92% to 95%, and careful swipes from 96% to 97%.
