Bigger emoji and GIF windows, a clearer GIF button, and zoom in Scan text.

### Changed
- **Taller emoji and GIF windows**: like Gboard, the emoji and GIF panels now open about 40% taller than the keys (never more than half the screen), so you see more at once. The keyboard goes back to its normal size when you return to the keys.
- **GIF button**: the toolbar now shows a clear "GIF" badge instead of the old diamond symbol.

### New
- **Zoom in Scan text**: pinch the photo, or tap the **+** and **−** buttons in its corner, to zoom in up to 6×. When zoomed, drag with one finger off the words (or two fingers anywhere) to move around. Tapping and dragging across words to select them works as before.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
