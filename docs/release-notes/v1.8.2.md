Typing right after a swipe no longer splits the word, so "i" after a swiped word becomes "I". Includes everything from 1.8.1.

### Fixed
- **The first letter you tap after a swiped word stays part of the word.** In apps like Google Keep, the first letter got cut off the word you were typing, so "if" became "i" + "f" and the bar suggested words for just "f". It also left "i" lowercase after a swiped word ("trying i", "hello i"), while it turned into "I" after a tapped word.
- From 1.8.1: a lone "i" becomes "I" in Keep and similar apps (⌫ right after turns it back), swipes use the word you just tapped as context, and fixing a swiped word teaches the right word pair.

Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
