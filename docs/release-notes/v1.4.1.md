Capital I, done right.

### Changed
- Only the word **I** on its own is always a capital. *i'm*, *i'll*, *i'd* and *i've* now follow the sentence: a capital at the start of a sentence, lowercase anywhere else.
- A predicted word you pick at the start of a sentence gets its capital letter.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
