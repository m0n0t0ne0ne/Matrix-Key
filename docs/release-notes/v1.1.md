Next-word predictions in more apps, and a vibration strength you can actually feel change.

### Fixed
- **Next-word prediction**: apps that ask keyboards for "no suggestions" (often just to hide spell-check underlines, as in some notes apps) now get suggestions and next-word predictions too. Autocorrect stays off in those fields, so nothing is changed without you tapping it.
- **Vibration strength**: every step of the slider now makes a clear difference. The lower part of the range uses the phone's crisp click effect, from a faint tap up to a full click; the upper part gives a firmer, longer thump.

### New
- **Vibration preview**: drag the *Vibration strength* slider in *Settings › Preferences* and you'll feel each strength as you go.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over 1.0 and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
