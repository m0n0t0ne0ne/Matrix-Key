Skin tones that are easier to pick.

### Fixed
- **The skin-tone picker stays open**: long-press an emoji and let go; the tones stay on screen until you tap one (or tap anywhere else to close them). You can still slide straight onto a tone and let go if you prefer.
- **Skin tones in emoji search**: long-press an emoji in the search results row to see its tones there; tap one to insert it, or **‹** to go back to the results. Your choice is remembered, as in the emoji panel.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
