Pick exactly which text to insert from a scan.

### New
- **Choose the words**: after you take or choose a photo, Scan text shows it with every word it read outlined. Everything starts selected; tap a word to add or remove it, or drag across the words you want to select just that run, like Gboard's scanner. **Select all** and **Clear** are right underneath, and the text box below still shows exactly what will be inserted so you can tweak it before tapping **Insert text**.
- The Take photo / Choose image page stays the same.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
