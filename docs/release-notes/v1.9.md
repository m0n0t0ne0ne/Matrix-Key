Swiping now follows your finger more closely, and you can rearrange the toolbar.

### New
- **Rearrange the toolbar.** Press and hold a toolbar button (GIF, clipboard, scan, settings, mic …) until it lifts, then drag it left or right and let go. The order is remembered. Buttons you've switched off keep their place for when you turn them back on.

### Changed
- **Swipes trust the path more than habit.** The more you used a word, the more it won swipes it didn't match, so you got "the" for "three", "something" for "showing", "fine" for "finger" or "did" for "code". The shape of your swipe now counts for more, and how often you use a word only helps up to a point. In tests built from your Nov 24 recordings, a heavy user's swipes were read right about 84% of the time, up from about 80%.
- A word you picked from the bar to fix a swipe gets a smaller lasting boost, so it no longer wins swipes that look like something else.

### Fixed
- **Deleting a swiped word right away (⌫) no longer teaches it.** Before, misreads you deleted, like "restricted" or "green", still counted as words you use.
- **A word swiped right after a period starts with a capital.** "up." then a swipe gave "up. instead"; it now gives "up. Instead".
