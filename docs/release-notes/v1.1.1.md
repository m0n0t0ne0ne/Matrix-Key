Vibration strength with a much wider range.

### Fixed
- **Vibration strength**: Android was scaling the keyboard's vibrations by the phone's own touch-feedback intensity, which squeezed the whole slider into a narrow, faint band. The slider now controls the strength directly, and both the power and the length of each tap grow with it: 0 is a faint tick, 100 a firm thump. The preview in *Settings › Preferences* uses the same scale.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
