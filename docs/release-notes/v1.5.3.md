Scan text: Insert text, take two.

### Fixed
- **Insert text** now hands the scanned text straight to the keyboard, which types it into your message box as soon as you're back in your app, instead of waiting for the keyboard to restart there. If the keyboard isn't open when you get back, the text is inserted the moment you tap the message box.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
