Skin tones for emoji, emoji search by group, and a capital I every time.

### New
- **Skin tones**: long-press any emoji of a person or hand (👦, 👍, 🙏, 💪 …) to pick the default yellow or one of five skin tones, light to dark. Slide to a tone and let go, or let go and tap one. Your choice is remembered, so that emoji shows in your tone from then on, in the panel and in search results.
- **Search by group**: searching *fruit* shows every fruit, *vegetables* every vegetable, and the same for *food*, *drinks*, *desserts*, *animals*, *birds*, *flowers*, *weather*, *sports*, *vehicles*, *hearts*, *hands*, *music*, *money* and more.

### Changed
- **"I" is always a capital**: *i*, *i'm*, *i'll*, *i'd* and *i've* are written with a capital I whether you type, swipe or pick them, even with autocorrect off.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
