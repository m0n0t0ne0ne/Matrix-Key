Swipe down to put the keyboard away.

### New
- **Swipe down to hide**: drag down on the suggestion bar or the toolbar and the keyboard closes. It works with an emoji, GIF or other panel open too, and any word you were typing is kept. Sideways swipes on the suggestion bar still scroll the suggestions.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
