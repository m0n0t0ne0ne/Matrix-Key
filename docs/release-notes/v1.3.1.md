Editing in search boxes, better emoji search, and fewer common words pushing out the one you meant.

### Fixed
- **Edit what you type in a search box**: in the emoji, GIF and AI prompt boxes you can now tap inside the text to put the cursor there, or slide on the space bar to move it letter by letter (with the usual haptic tick). Typing, swiping, backspace and swipe-delete all work at the cursor.
- **Emoji search finds more**: about 170 everyday words were added, such as *dad*, *mom*, *baby*, *happy*, *tired*, *party*, *luck*, *broken heart*, *beach*, *trophy*.
- **Less-common words**: very common words (*don't*, *the*) were winning too easily over less common ones you clearly swiped (*edit*). The shape of your swipe now counts for more. On simulated swipes of less common words, first-time accuracy went from about 84% to 90% for careful swipes and from 69% to 72% for sloppy ones, with common words unchanged.

### Install
Download the APK below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions and keeps your settings and dictionary. Requires Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone.
